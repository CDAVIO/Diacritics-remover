What are diacritics?

Diacritics are the small marks added to letters to change how they sound: the comma under ș and ț, the breve on ă, the circumflex on â and î, the umlaut on ü, the slash through ø and ł. Romanian has five letters with diacritics: ă, â, î, ș and ț.

Removing diacritics means replacing each marked letter with its plain Latin letter. “ș” becomes “s”, “ă” becomes “a”, “ü” becomes “u”, and the words stay easy to read: “Brașov” becomes “Brasov”, “Müller” becomes “Muller”.

Computers store these letters as special Unicode characters. Modern apps handle them well, but older systems, CSV files, SMS gateways and many forms only understand the 26 basic letters, known as ASCII. That is where text breaks, and where a diacritics remover helps.

When you should keep diacritics

Diacritics are part of correct Romanian. Keep them in official documents, contracts, books, articles and anywhere people read the text directly and the system supports them. Remove them only where they break: old systems, imports, file names, links, SMS and strict forms. DIACR never changes your original text, so you can keep both versions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a diacritics remover?

A diacritics remover turns letters with accents and marks, such as ș, ț, ă, é, ø or ü, into their plain versions: s, t, a, e, o, u. DIACR also cleans smart quotes, long dashes and invisible spaces.

Is DIACR free?

Yes. DIACR is free and open source under the MIT License. There is no account and no limit.

Does my text leave my device?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The page makes no network requests, so your text, files and clipboard never reach a server.

What is the difference between ș and ş?

ș and ț, with a comma below, are the correct Romanian letters. ş and ţ, with a cedilla, come from old fonts and keyboards. They look almost the same but are different characters, which is why search often fails. DIACR turns both into s and t, in lower and upper case.

Does it work for languages other than Romanian?

Yes. It handles Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Nordic languages, Czech, Croatian, Turkish, Icelandic and many more languages written in the Latin alphabet.

What happens to characters with no plain version, like € or Cyrillic letters?

They are left as they are and listed under the result, so nothing disappears without you knowing.

Can I clean a whole CSV or TXT file?

Yes. Upload a .txt or .csv file and DIACR downloads the cleaned version with the same file name. UTF-8 and older Windows-1250 files both work.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The page works in any modern mobile browser. Tap Paste and the clean text is copied for you.
