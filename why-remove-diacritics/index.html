Why remove diacritics? 15 real reasons

Letters like ș, ț, ă, â, î, ø, ü or ł are correct and beautiful, but many systems still break on them. Here are 15 everyday cases where removing diacritics saves time, money and embarrassment, each with a real example.

Email subjects and SMS cost more or look broken

“Programare confirmată” in a subject line can show as garbled symbols in some mail apps. In an SMS, a single “ă” switches the message to Unicode, which cuts it from 160 to 70 characters per message, so a campaign can cost twice as much.

Mixed legacy text with cedilla letters

Old Romanian documents use “ş ţ” (cedilla) while new ones use “ș ț” (comma). Search and “find and replace” treat them as different letters, so “Ştefan” isn’t found when you search for “Ștefan”. Removing both fixes it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a diacritics remover?

A diacritics remover turns letters with accents and marks, such as ș, ț, ă, é, ø or ü, into their plain versions: s, t, a, e, o, u. DIACR also cleans smart quotes, long dashes and invisible spaces.

Is DIACR free?

Yes. DIACR is free and open source under the MIT License. There is no account and no limit.

Does my text leave my device?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The page makes no network requests, so your text, files and clipboard never reach a server.

Does it handle both ș and ş, ț and ţ?

Yes. DIACR converts the correct comma-below letters and the old cedilla letters, in lower and upper case, so mixed old and new Romanian text comes out the same.

Can I clean a whole CSV or TXT file?

Yes. Upload a .txt or .csv file and DIACR downloads the cleaned version with the same file name. UTF-8 and older Windows-1250 files both work.

How do I use it fastest?

Open diacr.cdav.io and press Cmd + V or Ctrl + V anywhere on the page. The text is cleaned and the result is copied to your clipboard automatically.
