Why it happens

Many applicant tracking systems were built years ago for English text and store names in old encodings. When they meet ș, ț or ă they replace the letter with a question mark or a random symbol. The recruiter then sees a broken name, and search by name stops working.

Tips

  • Use the plain version of your name in portal fields and keep diacritics in the PDF CV, where fonts show them correctly.
  • Always write your email address without diacritics.
  • If a portal asks for a file name, use plain letters: CV_Stefan_Tandarica.pdf.

Questions

Will recruiters think my name is misspelled?

No. Romanian names without diacritics are common in international and online systems, and recruiters are used to them.

Should I remove diacritics from the whole CV?

Not necessarily. Keep them in the PDF if people read it, and remove them in portal forms, file names and plain text fields.
